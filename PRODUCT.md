# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Private households, families, seniors, and small businesses in Germany who need a reliable moving partner. They are usually comparing providers under time pressure and want to quickly understand whether Atlas Umzüge is professional, careful, insured, and easy to contact.

## Product Purpose

The website presents Atlas Umzüge as a premium moving service from Dornhan in the Schwarzwald. It should build trust quickly, explain the available moving, cleaning, clearance, connection, and protection services clearly, and convert visitors into quote requests through WhatsApp, phone calls, and direct contact.

Success means visitors feel confident that Atlas Umzüge will handle their move carefully and professionally, then take action by requesting a quote or calling.

## Brand Personality

Calm, premium, trustworthy. The brand should feel structured, careful, local, and high-end without becoming cold or overly corporate.

## Anti-references

Avoid cheap discount-mover aesthetics, cluttered sales-page design, loud urgency banners, generic stock-heavy layouts, gimmicky animations, overdesigned glass effects, and anything that makes the service feel careless or low-cost.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with trust before persuasion: every section should reduce uncertainty about reliability, care, safety, and communication.
2. Keep the experience spacious and structured: premium service should feel calm, not busy.
3. Make action paths obvious: quote request, WhatsApp, and phone contact should be easy to reach without overpowering the page.
4. Show competence through details: spacing, icon treatment, motion, image cropping, and copy should all feel deliberate.
5. Preserve the Atlas identity: burgundy, warm neutrals, dark brown, Inter typography, and real moving-service imagery remain the core system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA where practical for contrast, focus visibility, keyboard access, and readable text sizing. Respect reduced-motion preferences, keep touch targets at least 44px on mobile, and ensure essential contact actions remain accessible on small screens.
